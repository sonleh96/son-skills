## Son helper deviation

The editable Son orchestration helper intentionally differs from the imported Pstack helper at `skills/son-mode/scripts/orch/store.ts`.
When `gh` or Origin returns historical closed or merged rows alongside one open row for a branch, the resolver selects the sole open row regardless of response order.
More than one open row remains ambiguous and fails.
With no open row, one historical row remains a valid anchor and multiple historical rows still fail.
This fixes the real `--state all` forge response without changing the immutable upstream snapshot or candidate manifest.
The helper is not a leaf original, so its intentional local deviation is recorded here rather than represented by an `imported_files` hash in `stack.json`.
