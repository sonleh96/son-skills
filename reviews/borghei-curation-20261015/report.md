# Borghei skills curation review

Keep Son's existing orchestration and pstack principles, and borrow only methods that add a distinct, recurring outcome.
The initial shortlist is two conditional pilots: `research/litreview` and `business-operations/process-mapper`.
Keep `c-level-advisor/board-deck-builder` for a second wave and use PRFAQ as an optional reference in the existing product-planning playbook.
No new global principles or agent personas are warranted by this review.
Nothing was imported or installed.

The review covers all 385 real skills at Borghei commit `4a698e8d07856fdca9ec3e69082a9d68c8dd45d0` and excludes one sample fixture.
The comparison baseline has 103 curated Son skills, 29 playbooks, and 24 pstack principles, plus separately installed specialists and plugins.
Seventy-one complete skill bodies were read, including 27 packages with targeted supporting-file inspection; the other 314 received metadata screening.
The merged evidence check verified 168 cited ranges across 105 pinned source files.
No creator code ran, and static inspection does not establish runtime behavior or complete package safety.

These are curation decisions, not 385 safety verdicts.

A skill package and a playbook occupy different layers.
A preserved source package supplies a bounded method, references, and optional tools.
A Son playbook decides when that method is useful, what evidence it needs, and where the result goes next.
A pstack principle governs behavior across unrelated tasks.
A role supplies expertise without taking routing or authority away from `son-agent`.

Require a missing outcome, compare methods with existing suppliers, separate guidance from helper reliability, and prefer optional stages before new playbooks.
Rewriting scoring, inference, or validation logic is substantive maintenance work, not a cosmetic adapter.

No new global principle emerged.
The useful general rules already map to pstack: prove-it-works covers verification evidence; explain-the-number covers thresholds and quantitative claims; subtract-before-you-add covers duplicate suppliers; encode-lessons-in-structure covers repeatable checklists and records; sequence-verifiable-units covers delivery slices; and test-behavior-not-implementation covers meaningful evaluation.
Domain checklists, role perspectives, fixed scoring weights, and templates should stay local to their tasks.
The review does not turn Borghei's universal scores, slide counts, utilization bands, response times, or approval tiers into Son policy.

Defer a11y-audit and doc-drift-detector because their static checks overstate rendered accessibility and link verification.
Reject prompt-governance as a default because its lexical safety verdicts and catalog writes introduce unresolved problems.
Use skill-security-auditor only as a manual package-review reference.
Keep `agentic-evaluation-framework`, `data-quality-auditor`, and `runbook-generator` as bounded workflow references rather than validated helper suites.

Ignore broad replacements for existing engineering roles, PM frameworks, artifact creation, writing policy, and agent coordination.
Keep the current Borghei-derived `predictive-modeling` and canonical VoltAgent `data-scientist` and `ml-engineer` choices.
Do not multiply them with senior-data-scientist, senior-ml-engineer, another company OS, router, self-improvement loop, persona hierarchy, or model-effort policy.
Son already owns those decisions.
Defer legal, regulatory, clinical, tenant administration, grants, commercial approval, and vertical-advisor packages until a concrete task establishes jurisdiction, authority, inputs, and a missing method.

Supplier boundaries also affect this decision.
A capability in the 103-skill curated baseline is different from a specialist observed installed on this machine.
PM methods, native analytics validation, Documents, PDF, Spreadsheets, Presentations, and connectors are separately provisioned suppliers unless explicitly included in Son's installation contract.
When writing a playbook, name and detect the required supplier.
If portability later demands an original Borghei replacement, select a small replacement set rather than retaining two defaults for the same capability.

A later adoption should preserve the original folder, references, assets, license, and pinned provenance.
An adapter may disable an unreliable helper; it cannot make that helper accurate by changing its description.
Evaluate the two pilots on real tasks before adding them to the accepted set.
